Good to Know

A few common questions

Do you provide the alcohol?

No. Florida treats bartending as a service, not a sale, so we bring the bartender, the ice, the mixers, and the garnish, and you supply the spirits, beer, or wine. We are happy to help you figure out exactly what and how much to buy for your guest count.

What is included in a package?

Every package includes a professional bartender, a custom cocktail menu built around your event, bar tools, ice, mixers, fresh garnish, and full setup and cleanup. You supply the alcohol and the guest list, we handle everything else.

How far do you travel?

We travel throughout the Miami area at no extra charge within a 20 mile radius. Events farther out may include a small travel fee, which we will always confirm with you before booking.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A deposit secures your date and is applied toward your final total. The remaining balance is due before or on the day of your event.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Reach out as soon as you can and we will do our best to move your date. Deposits hold your spot on the calendar, so they are generally not refundable, but we will always work with you.

Do you offer alcohol free options?

Absolutely. We are glad to build alcohol free or low proof drinks into your menu for guests who want something just as crafted, without the alcohol.

How many guests can you serve?

1 bartender comfortably serves up to about 75 guests. For larger events, we bring on a barback for extra support, and once you pass 120 guests, we add a second bartender to keep the line moving.
