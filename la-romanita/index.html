Our Story

Two capitals,
one table.

La Romanita is the first restaurant from Prestige Hospitality Group, at the heart of Hialeah's Amelia District. The idea is simple and personal: the Italian table our grandparents dreamed about, cooked with the Cuban flavors we grew up on.

Executive Chef Reinier "Rey" Mendez was born in Havana, where he started out running a pizzeria. Kitchens at STK Miami, Shula's Steakhouse, Casa Tua Cucina and Lure Fish Bar followed. Here he brings it home: vaca frita on a crisp crust, oxtail folded into risotto, guava in the torrejas.

“Italian technique, Cuban soul. Every plate should feel like Sunday at abuela's, but taste like a night in Rome.”
