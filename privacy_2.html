Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

This page explains what information Heavens Bartending collects through this website and how that information is used. It applies only to this website and its inquiry form.

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through this site, we collect the information you choose to share, which may include your name, phone number, event date, and any details about your event you include in the message field.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, discuss your event, and coordinate booking details. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third Party Services

Our inquiry form is processed through Formspree, a third party form service that delivers your submission to our inbox. Formspree handles the technical delivery of your message and maintains its own privacy practices, which you can review at their website.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and coordinate your event, and generally delete older inquiries that did not result in a booking.

Your Choices

If you would like your information updated or removed, email us at heavensbartending@gmail.com and we will take care of it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to heavensbartending@gmail.com.
